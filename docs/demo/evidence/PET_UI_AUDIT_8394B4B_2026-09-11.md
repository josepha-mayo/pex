# PEX two-pet UI and atlas audit — 11 September 2026

Product source: `8394b4b5cbaf154e6a1aafa29193497957f1d0c2`.

## Overlay repair

The floating companion previously rendered the sprite at a user-controlled
scale while keeping the actor width, status-bubble allocation and hide-button
anchor fixed at the one-times geometry. At the supported 0.8–1.4 UI range this
could make the hide control drift over or away from the pet and could squeeze
or clip the adjacent status bubble.

`PetStage` now derives one overlay actor-width variable from the same scale used
by the sprite. The actor, remaining bubble width and fixed hide-button anchor all
consume that value. The status-message dismiss button remains a separate control;
the pet hide button remains available independently of the message.
